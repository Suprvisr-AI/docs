# Upgrading your plan

## What it does

Walks you through choosing a paid plan after your trial, including how to apply a discount code if you have one.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Billing** (or click the **Choose a Plan** button on the trial banner).
2. On the upgrade page, choose **Annual** or **Monthly** at the top.
3. Adjust the **Seat slider** to your team size.
4. Compare **Teams** and **Pro** side by side. Pro adds Agent Panel, VERA scoring, advanced models, unlimited workspaces, and more.
5. Click **Choose Teams** or **Choose Pro**.
6. On Stripe Checkout, fill in payment details. If you have a **discount code** and didn't open a personalized link, type it in the "Add promotion code" field.
7. Complete payment.

You return to Suprvisr with all paid features unlocked.

<Callout type="info">
**Have a personalized link with a coupon?** Open it directly — the discount is pre-applied. You'll see a small "Discount code applied" chip on the upgrade page before you hit Stripe.
</Callout>

## Switching plans later

- Open **Settings → Billing → Manage Billing** to change seats, switch annual/monthly, or cancel.
- Teams users can move to Pro at any time from the same page; click **Upgrade to Pro**.

## What happens to your data

- All chats, projects, and uploads are preserved.
- Workspace counts above the new plan's cap stay accessible. You just can't create new workspaces until you archive down.
