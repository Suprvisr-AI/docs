# Image Generation

**What it does**: Ask Cumulus to generate an image and it creates one right in the chat. Images render inline with a lightbox view and download button. Each user gets a weekly quota.

---

## How to use it

You can trigger image generation in two ways:

**Slash command** — type `/image` followed by a description:
- `/image a modern office workspace with natural lighting`
- `/image a minimalist logo concept for a coffee brand`

**Natural language** — just ask in any chat:
- "Generate an image of a team brainstorming session"
- "Create an illustration of our product roadmap"

The AI uses its image generation tool automatically when your message calls for a visual. You'll see a **"Generating image..."** indicator while it works.

---

## Viewing and downloading

Generated images appear inline in the chat. You can:

- **Click the image** to open a full-size lightbox view
- **Download** using the download button on the image

Each image shows the prompt that was used and your usage count (e.g. "2/10 this week").

---

## Weekly quota

Each user has a weekly image generation limit. Your current usage is shown on every generated image as a counter (e.g. "3/10 this week").

When you reach your limit, a **"Weekly limit reached"** card appears instead of generating an image. The card shows your usage and a progress bar. Your quota resets automatically each week.

<Callout type="info">
  Image generation limits are set at the organization level. If you need a higher limit, ask your admin about upgrading your plan.
</Callout>

---

## Admin controls

Image generation is controlled at the organization level. Admins can enable or disable it from **Settings > Organization**.

When disabled, the `/image` slash command is hidden and the AI will not generate images even if asked directly.

---

## Tips & gotchas

- **Be descriptive** — the more detail you give in your prompt, the better the result
- **Quota is per user** — your usage doesn't affect teammates
- **Images are stored** — generated images persist in your chat history and can be downloaded later
- **Signed URLs** — image links expire after 7 days, but Cumulus automatically refreshes them when you revisit the chat
