# Pricing & Plans

## What it does

Suprvisr offers a 30-day **Free Trial**, a **Teams** plan ($30/seat/mo), and a **Pro** plan ($55/seat/mo). All paid plans bill per seat with monthly or annual options. Annual saves vs monthly.

## Plans at a glance

| | Free Trial | Teams | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price/seat/mo** | Free | $30 ($25 annual) | $55 ($45 annual) |
| **Members** | Up to 3 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Workspaces** | 3 | 3 | Unlimited |
| **Storage** | 1 GB | 10 GB | 20 GB |
| **Duration** | 30 days | Recurring | Recurring |
| **Feature access** | Pro-equivalent | Teams set | Full set |

## What's in each tier

**Available in Teams + Pro (and Free Trial):**
- AI Assistant + document upload + knowledge base
- Image generation
- AI memory & personalization
- Web search
- Project member roles (Owner / Editor / Member)
- 48-hour ticketing support

**Pro-only (and Free Trial):**
- **Agent Panel** — autonomous task execution
- Scheduled tasks
- VERA scoring & VHR Report
- Advanced AI models + model switching
- Extended thinking (deep reasoning)
- SharePoint & Google Drive sync
- Workspace threads (collaborative chat)
- Unlimited workspaces · 20 GB storage
- Organizational dashboards
- 24-hour priority support

## How to upgrade

1. From any page, open **Settings → Billing**.
2. Click **Choose a Plan** (or **Upgrade to Pro** if you're already on Teams).
3. Pick Teams or Pro on the comparison page.
4. Stripe Checkout opens — complete payment.

You can change seat count, switch annual/monthly, or cancel from the **Manage Billing** portal at any time.

## Discount codes

If you have a discount code, you can either type it on the Stripe Checkout page (look for "Add promotion code") or open a personalized link with the code in the URL: `https://app.suprvisr.ai/billing/upgrade?coupon=YOUR_CODE`. Personalized links pre-apply the discount and show a "Discount code applied" chip on the upgrade page before you head to Stripe.

<Callout type="warning">
Your trial gives full Pro access for 30 days. After that, you must pick a plan to keep using AI features.
</Callout>

## Workspace limits

Teams plans cap at 3 workspaces; Pro is unlimited. If you create more than 3 workspaces during your trial and then choose Teams, all of them stay accessible — but you can't create new ones until you archive down to 2 active. Existing data is never deleted.
