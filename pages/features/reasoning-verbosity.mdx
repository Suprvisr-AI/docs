# Reasoning

Cumulus lets you control how deeply the AI thinks before responding. Choose a reasoning level to balance speed against thoroughness — from instant answers to multi-step analysis.

---

## Reasoning levels

Click the **sliders icon** in the bottom-right of the chat input to open the settings panel. Under **Reasoning**, choose a level:

| Level | Description | Best for |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Minimal** | Faster responses | Quick questions, simple lookups, casual conversation |
| **Low** | Some reasoning | Everyday tasks, drafting, summarizing |
| **Medium** | Balanced depth | Multi-step problems, analysis, research |
| **High** | Deeper analysis | Complex technical questions, strategic decisions, reconciling multiple sources |

Your selection applies to the next message you send. The panel closes automatically after you choose.

<Callout type="info">
  The default is **Minimal**. The AI still provides thoughtful answers at this level — higher settings add explicit step-by-step reasoning before the response.
</Callout>

---

## When to increase reasoning

Higher reasoning levels are most useful when:

- The question requires **reconciling multiple documents** or data sources
- You need the AI to **show its work** — visible reasoning steps in the thinking panel
- The problem has **multiple valid approaches** and you want the AI to evaluate trade-offs
- Accuracy matters more than speed

<Callout type="tip">
  For simple questions ("What's our refund policy?"), Minimal is best. For complex questions ("Compare our Q3 and Q4 results and recommend budget adjustments"), try Medium or High.
</Callout>

---

## Reasoning visibility

Regardless of the level you choose, you can see the AI's reasoning process in the **thinking panel** during generation. This shows what the AI is working through, what sources it's pulling from, and tool call results as they happen.
