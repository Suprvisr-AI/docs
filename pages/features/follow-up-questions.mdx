# Follow-Up Questions

**What it does**: When the AI needs more information to give you a great answer, it pauses and asks targeted clarifying questions in an interactive card — then picks up right where it left off with your answers as context.

---

## How it works

Instead of guessing what you mean, the AI can ask 1–4 follow-up questions before proceeding. This typically happens when you ask it to create, write, or analyze something without specifying key details like topic, audience, format, or which data source to use.

<Steps>

### The AI detects ambiguity

You send a message like "Write a proposal for the team" — the AI recognizes it needs more context (who's the audience? what's the topic? what format?).

### A follow-up card appears

A multi-question stepper card shows up in your chat. Each question includes 2–4 preset answer options to choose from, plus a free-text field if you'd rather type your own answer.

### Answer the questions

Select a preset option or type a custom answer. The card automatically advances to the next question. You can also **skip** individual questions or **skip all** remaining questions if you'd rather let the AI decide.

### The AI continues

Once you've answered (or skipped), the AI resumes its work using your answers as context. Your follow-up responses are woven into the conversation so the AI produces exactly what you had in mind.

</Steps>

---

<Callout type="tip">
  **Pro tip**: If the AI asks a question you don't have a strong opinion on, just skip it. The AI will make a reasonable default choice and keep moving.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  **Note**: The AI won't ask follow-ups for simple questions, greetings, or factual lookups — only when your request could go in meaningfully different directions and clarification would improve the result.
</Callout>

---

## What the card looks like

- **Progress indicator** — shows "1 of 3" so you know how many questions are left
- **Preset options** — numbered buttons for common answers (click to select and auto-advance)
- **Custom input** — free-text field for anything not covered by the presets
- **Navigation** — Back button to revisit previous answers, Skip to move on
- **Collapsed state** — after answering, the card collapses to a compact summary showing your responses. Click to expand and review the full Q&A

---

## Tips & gotchas

- **Answers persist across page refreshes** — your follow-up state is saved, so you won't lose progress if you reload the page
- **One follow-up at a time** — the AI won't chain multiple rounds of follow-ups. It asks everything it needs in a single card.
- **Skipping is fine** — skipped questions don't block the AI. It proceeds with whatever context it has.
- **Review your answers** — answered follow-up cards stay in the chat as collapsed summaries. Expand them anytime to see what you told the AI.
