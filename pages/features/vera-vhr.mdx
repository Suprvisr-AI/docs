# VHR & VERA — Productivity Metrics

**What it does**: VHR (Value Hours Recovered) measures how much time your team saves using AI. VERA (Verified Effort Recovery Assessment) is the algorithm that calculates VHR — designed to be credible, transparent, and auditable.

---

## How it works

VERA scores every AI response individually, based on what the AI actually did — not just how many tokens it generated.

<Steps>

### Task classification

Each AI response is classified by what it accomplished:

| Type | What it means | Time credit |
|---|---|---|
| **Trivial** | Very short response, no context used | 0 min |
| **Lookup** | Quick fact or answer | 5 min |
| **Drafting** | Content creation (emails, docs) | 15 min |
| **Analysis** | Research, synthesis, or multi-turn work | 30 min |
| **Generation** | Charts, images, or structured reports | 35 min |

### Context multiplier

When the AI uses your company's documents (via knowledge base or project files), the score increases:

- **No company docs**: 1.0x
- **1-2 document chunks**: 1.25x
- **3+ chunks** (cross-document synthesis): 1.5x

### Action premiums

Certain high-value outputs earn additional credit:

| Action | Premium |
|---|---|
| Chart or visualization | +20 min |
| Image generated | +20 min |
| Structured report (SOP, export) | +25 min |
| Multi-turn conversation (3+ turns) | +8 min |
| File analyzed | +15 min |

### Credibility decay

To prevent inflated numbers, VERA reduces credit for high-volume query days. Power users doing deep work (RAG, tools, analysis) get wider brackets before decay kicks in.

</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  **Calibration**: VERA's time credits are set at 40-50% of manual task benchmarks from McKinsey, Harvard/BCG, Forrester, and other studies. This reflects that AI handles first-draft generation while humans still review and refine.
</Callout>

## Reading the VHR Report

The VHR Report (admin only) has three layers:

### Dashboard

- **VHR Yield**: Hours recovered per paid seat, annualized. This is the single number that tells you how much value your org is getting.
- **Tier label**: At Risk (&lt; 60), Industry Average (60-120), Strong Adoption (120-200), or Best-in-Class (200+).
- **Trend chart**: VHR over time — is adoption growing, plateauing, or declining?
- **Task type breakdown**: What kinds of work is the AI doing for your team?
- **Per-user table**: Individual VHR for every active user.

### How VERA Works

An expandable panel explaining the full formula, scoring tiers, and calibration methodology. Show this to your CFO when they ask how the numbers are calculated.

### Audit drill-down

Click any user to see their daily breakdown. Click a day to see individual messages. Click a message to see exactly how its VERA score was calculated — task type, multiplier, premiums, decay rate, and final score.

<Callout type="tip">
  **Feedback matters**: When a user gives a thumbs-down on an AI response, that message's VHR score is automatically zeroed. This keeps the metric honest — only valued responses count.
</Callout>

---

## Tips & gotchas

- **VHR is time-only**: We report hours saved, not dollar values. Leadership can infer value based on their own context.
- **No individual rankings**: Admins see per-user VHR in a table, but there are no leaderboards or rankings. Users draw their own conclusions.
- **Scores are per-message**: Each AI response gets its own VERA score at creation time. You can audit any individual score.
- **Deep work is rewarded**: The depth-aware decay system means power users doing real analytical work aren't penalized the same as someone sending many short queries.
